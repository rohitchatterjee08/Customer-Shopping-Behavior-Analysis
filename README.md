# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** that analyzes customer shopping behavior to uncover trends in purchasing patterns, customer segments, product performance, discounts, subscriptions, and revenue.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow — from **data preparation in Python** and **SQL-based business analysis in PostgreSQL** to an interactive **Power BI dashboard**, project report, and presentation.

---

## 📌 Overview

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior to improve **sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty**.

The key business question addressed in this project is:

> **How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

The dataset contains **3,900 purchase records and 18 features**, covering customer demographics, purchase details, and shopping behavior.

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Records:** 3,900
**Features:** 18

The dataset contains information related to:

* Customer demographics
* Purchase amounts
* Product categories
* Discounts
* Reviews and ratings
* Shipping methods
* Subscription status
* Previous purchases
* Purchase frequency
* Customer behavior

There were **37 missing values in Review Rating**, which were handled during data preparation.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading & Exploration — Python

The dataset was loaded using Pandas and explored using:

* `head()`
* `info()`
* `describe()`
* Missing-value analysis
* Column inspection
* Exploratory Data Analysis

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv("customer_shopping_behavior.csv")

df.head()
df.info()
df.describe(include="all")
```

### 2. Data Cleaning & Transformation

The following preparation steps were performed:

* Handled missing review ratings using **category-level median imputation**
* Standardized column names using `snake_case`
* Renamed columns for easier analysis
* Created an `age_group` feature
* Converted purchase frequency into number of days
* Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column
* Prepared the cleaned dataset for database analysis

---

### 3. PostgreSQL Database Integration

The cleaned Pandas DataFrame was loaded into **PostgreSQL** using SQLAlchemy.

The cleaned data was stored in a `customer` table and used for further business analysis.

---

### 4. SQL Business Analysis

SQL queries were created to answer practical business questions, including:

* Revenue generated by gender
* High-spending customers using discounts
* Top-rated products
* Standard vs. Express shipping spending
* Subscriber vs. non-subscriber behavior
* Products with the highest discount rates
* Customer segmentation
* Top products within each category
* Repeat buyers and subscription behavior
* Revenue contribution by age group

## 📈 Key Results & Insights

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

* **3,116 customers** were classified as Loyal.
* **701 customers** were classified as Returning.
* **83 customers** were classified as New.
* Clothing generated the highest number of purchases among the analyzed categories.
* **839 high-spending discount users** were identified.
* Hats had the highest discount-dependent purchase rate among the analyzed products.
* Male customers generated higher total revenue than female customers in the dataset.
* Subscribers and non-subscribers showed very similar average spending.
* The analysis highlighted opportunities to strengthen subscriptions and loyalty programs.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

* **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits and incentives for subscribers.
* **Strengthen Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat customers to increase long-term retention.
* **Review Discount Strategy:** Balance increased sales with potential impact on profit margins.
* **Improve Product Positioning:** Highlight highly rated and best-selling products.
* **Targeted Marketing:** Focus campaigns on high-revenue customer segments and relevant purchasing behaviors.
